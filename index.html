How this check works

This prototype flags configured approved facts that are missing and checks for configured phrases your team has marked as unsupported. Add your real approved facts in Brand Settings. This is a rule-based demo, not an AI fact-verification service.

Prototype limitations

This browser-based MVP stores data locally on this device. It does not yet include secure team accounts, cloud storage, PDF guideline ingestion, live GPT/Gemini analysis, ChromaDB or LangChain. Those require a separately hosted backend and API credentials. Do not upload confidential company content to this demo.
